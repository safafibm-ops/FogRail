    The cockpit idea

    Pilots land blind. So can dumpers.
    • Synthetic viewThe HUD draws the road as it looks on a clear day, from a 3D twin rebuilt by fleet dashcams in every clear hour, placed by RTK GNSS + IMU. Live objects come only from radar and V2V.
    • Fleet radarEvery truck's 4D radar feeds one shared picture of the road. A truck coming down the hairpin clears it for the truck going up.
    • Fog TrainTrucks in V2V contact couple like train cars: brake intent arrives in 0.3 s instead of a 1.5 s human reaction, so gaps shrink safely and a whole train passes the hairpin on one token.
    • Weather meshEvery truck measures visibility. The control room gets a live fog map and a per-segment nowcast.
    • Safety coreVirtual blocks, safe-speed governor and the fail-safe ladder stay deterministic and fully offline. No roadside poles.
    Simulated prototype for demo. The synthetic view, radar cones and nowcast are simulated from the sim's own map and fog model. Geometry, fleet size, grades and speed limits are illustrative, not NMDC survey data. 100 t payload per dumper. Vision-only baseline uses the same fleet and fog, with speed limited by sight distance.
